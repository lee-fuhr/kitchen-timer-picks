A reliable kitchen timer is one of the cheapest insurance policies you can buy. Whether you’re simmering stock, proofing bread, or roasting vegetables, a timer frees you from staring at the clock and helps you nail doneness every time. Budget-friendly options are plentiful, but not all are built to last or easy to set in a hurry. When choosing a budget timer, focus on three things: a clear, loud enough beep (that you can hear over a range hood), intuitive buttons or dials, and reasonable battery life.
